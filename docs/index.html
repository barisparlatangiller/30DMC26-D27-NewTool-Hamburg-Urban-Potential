Homes or workshops?

One Hamburg block, 26 parcels: how much more could be built here, and who would it be for?

Hamburg finished about 6,000 new flats in 2025. Adding floors to existing buildings is one way to find more room without new land. This tool tests that on one ordinary block in Barmbek-Süd, against what its local plan (B-Plan Barmbek-Süd 42, 2019) allows.

    Method and limits

    Plan. The plan sheet (Bezirksamt Hamburg-Nord, 2019) was fitted to the ALKIS parcels (accuracy ≈ 1 m) and the zones (WA/GE with GRZ, GFZ), building limits (Baugrenzen) and storey limits were digitised from it. The XPlanGML service had no geometry for this plan.

    Existing. LoD2 building models (LGV Hamburg): footprint × storeys (from the model, otherwise eave height ÷ 3 m).

    On paper = what the numbers allow minus what exists: floor area GFZ × parcel, or, without a GFZ, GRZ × parcel × storey limit.

    Scenarios keep every building standing and never exceed what is free on paper. One more storey: +1 storey on every building below its limit. Roofs + street gaps: rooftops up to the limit (max +2, one fewer on pitched roofs) and new buildings only in gaps within 16 m of the street; yards stay free. Maximum: also new buildings in the yards.

    Common rules: new buildings only on unbuilt ground inside the building limits, 3 m clear of existing buildings, at least 8 m wide and 120 m², within the GRZ still free; no rooftop storeys on buildings under 60 m² or on the listed building.

    Not checked: distances between buildings (Abstandsflächen, HBauO), structure of each roof, how yards are used (delivery, parking), trees on private ground, ownership and economics. Treat the scenarios as a range, not a forecast.

    People. 78% of the gross floor area becomes living space; one flat per 60 m² of it, 1.6 people per flat; a new storey is about 3 m (habitable rooms need 2.40 m clear height, HBauO § 47). Homes only on residential parcels (WA): the commercial zone (GE) does not allow them (BauNVO § 8), so its new space is shown as workplaces, ≈ 32 m² gross each if it were offices. The MU switch shows what the block could hold if homes were allowed there too (an upper bound: MU asks for a mix). HBauO eases rooftop flats: no lift and no barrier-free flats required for up to two added storeys (§ 39, § 50).

    ⚠️ A scenario on the published plan, not a planning or legal assessment.
