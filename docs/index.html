    Method and limits

    Plan. The plan sheet (Bezirksamt Hamburg-Nord, 2019) was fitted to the ALKIS parcels (accuracy ≈ 1 m) and the zones (WA/GE with GRZ, GFZ), building limits (Baugrenzen) and storey limits were digitised from it. The XPlanGML service had no geometry for this plan.

    Existing. LoD2 building models (LGV Hamburg): footprint × storeys (from the model, otherwise eave height ÷ 3 m).

    Theoretical = what the numbers allow minus what exists: floor area GFZ × parcel, or, without a GFZ, GRZ × parcel × storey limit.

    Feasible keeps today's buildings standing: new building only on unbuilt ground inside the building limits (3 m clear of existing buildings, at least 8 m wide and 120 m²) and within the GRZ still free; rooftop extensions up to the storey limit but at most +2 storeys, one fewer on pitched roofs (the attic already counts), none on buildings under 60 m²; the listed building stays as it is.

    Homes ≈ gross floor area ÷ 90 m², residential zones only. GRZ overruns for parking (§19(4) BauNVO), noise rules, distances, ownership and economics are left out.

    ⚠️ A scenario on the published plan, not a planning or legal assessment.
